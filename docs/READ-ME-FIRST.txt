CUTLINE 1.0.0
Video editor for cutting long footage into finished clips. Works fully offline: no account, no
internet connection, nothing you make leaves your computer.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a 64-bit Linux desktop. 8 GB of memory recommended.
  A graphics card with WebGL2 support is recommended. Without it Cutline still runs, with fewer
  effects in preview and export.


INSTALL
  Windows installer: double-click Cutline-Setup-1.0.0.exe and follow the steps. Windows may show
  "Windows protected your PC" the first time; click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  Windows portable: double-click Cutline-Portable-1.0.0.exe. Nothing is installed.
  Linux: extract the archive, then run the "cutline" file inside the extracted folder. If it does
  not start, run it from a terminal with: ./cutline --no-sandbox


TRIAL AND LICENCE KEY
  Cutline is fully usable without a key. Exported videos and images then carry a small Cutline
  watermark. To remove it, buy a licence key, then in Cutline open Help > About and Licence, paste
  the key and click Activate. Activation works offline. A key is for you alone, on up to three of
  your own computers (see EULA.txt).


FIRST EDIT IN FIVE STEPS
  1. Click Import in the Media panel (or drag files onto it). MP4, MOV, M4A, WAV, PNG, JPEG and
     WebP files are supported.
  2. Double-click a file to open it in the Source monitor. Press I and O to mark the part you want.
  3. Click Insert or Overwrite to put it on the timeline at the playhead.
  4. Cut with Ctrl+K at the playhead, or the Razor tool (C). Select a clip to change its picture,
     effects, sound and speed in the Inspector. Add titles with Ctrl+T and captions from the
     Titles menu (SRT and VTT files can be imported).
  5. Press Ctrl+E to export: MP4, WebM, a PNG frame or WAV audio, at the size you choose.
  Save your project with Ctrl+S. Cutline also autosaves and offers to recover after a crash.


GOOD TO KNOW
  - Projects (.cutline files) store where your media files are, not copies of them. If you move
    media, Cutline marks it offline; import the same file again and it is relinked.
  - WebM and MKV files cannot be imported; convert them to MP4 first.
  - Not included in this version: multicam, motion tracking, plugins (VST, OFX), nested sequences,
    HDR and 10-bit, camera RAW, cloud or collaboration features, AI features.
  - Help > Export Error Log saves a file you can send to the seller if something goes wrong.
